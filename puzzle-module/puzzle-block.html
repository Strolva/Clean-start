📖 How to Play & Game Modes
  • Load or Record: Tap Load Audio to pick a sound file, or tap Record to capture your microphone.
  • Slide to Solve: Tap any tile directly next to the empty dashed slot to slide it into place and hear its audio slice.
  • Check Your Progress: Tiles glow with a blue border when placed in their correct spot. Use Ref Audio anytime to hear the full track.
  • Difficulty Modes:
    • Easy: Combine custom images with audio and a faint background guide.
    • Medium: Rebuild the track using sliced purple audio waveforms.
    • Hard: Pure ear training—no visual clues on the tiles!
